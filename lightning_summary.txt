Lightning seen from the International Space Station, March 2017 to July 2023.
There is one map for each month: 77 months.
Numbers go from 0 (no lightning) to 1 (the busiest place in the busiest month).

Key facts:
- The stormiest place on Earth in this data is the Congo rainforest.
- Other lightning hot spots: Central Africa, Gabon and Cameroon, Colombia and Venezuela, the central United States.
- Worldwide, the most lightning comes in June and August, during summer in the northern half of the world.
- The quietest months are January and February.
- The busiest single month was 2018-08. The quietest was 2020-01.
- Lightning is mostly over land, not over the ocean.
- The space station cannot see the far north or far south, so those areas have no data.
